A free, offline Typeless alternative for Mac

Typeless and Tidytalk both turn natural speech into polished text, removing fillers and fixing self-corrections. Typeless does it in the cloud with an account and a subscription. Tidytalk does it on your Mac, for free, with no account.

Updated 2026-10-19

Tidytalk is our project, so we've kept this page factual. Typeless details come from its official pricing and data controls pages, checked in October 2026. Products change, so check their site before you decide.

Typeless says audio isn't stored and its AI providers don't keep or train on your data. With Tidytalk, audio never leaves your Mac in the first place.

What works the same

  • Speak naturally in any app and get clean text: fillers and repetitions removed, self-corrections resolved, punctuation added.
  • A personal dictionary for names and terms.
  • Speak one language and get text in another.
  • Edit text by voice: select it and say what to change.

When Typeless is the better choice

  • You want the same dictation on Windows, Linux, or your phone.
  • You'd rather not set anything up: no model download and no AI service to choose.
  • You use an Intel Mac or a macOS version older than 26.

When Tidytalk is the better choice

  • You don't want your voice to leave your Mac, or you need dictation that works offline.
  • You dictate a lot and don't want a weekly word limit, a subscription, or an account.
  • You speak Chinese mixed with English. Tidytalk keeps the English terms and adds Chinese–English spacing.
  • You also want live captions for videos and meetings, or transcription of recordings, at no cost.

Switching from Typeless

  1. Download Tidytalk and follow the setup guide.
  2. Copy the words from your Typeless dictionary into Settings › Vocabulary › Common Words.
  3. Choose an AI service in Settings › Set Up AI. Ollama, LM Studio, or Apple Intelligence keep everything on your Mac.
  4. Pick a shortcut in Settings › Dictation. Voice editing of selected text uses Right Option by default.
