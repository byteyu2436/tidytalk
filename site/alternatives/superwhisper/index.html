A free, open-source Superwhisper alternative for Mac

Superwhisper and Tidytalk both turn speech into text at your cursor and can run fully on your Mac. The difference: in Tidytalk, AI cleanup is free, works with any local or cloud model in any language, and the code is open source.

Updated 2026-10-19

Tidytalk is our project, so we've kept this page factual. Superwhisper details come from its official documentation, checked in October 2026. Products change, so check their site before you decide.

Both have custom vocabulary and text replacements. Tidytalk also includes live captions with translation for any audio your Mac plays, and transcription of files and YouTube or Bilibili links, all free.

When Superwhisper is the better choice

  • You want the same app on Windows, iPhone, or Android.
  • You want many customizable AI modes, a meeting assistant, or team features like SSO.
  • You use an Intel Mac or a macOS version older than 26.

When Tidytalk is the better choice

  • You want AI cleanup without paying for a subscription or a license.
  • You want cleanup to stay on your Mac in Chinese, Japanese, or another language, not just English.
  • You speak Chinese mixed with English. Tidytalk's default model is built for it and adds Chinese–English spacing.
  • You want to read the code or change it yourself.

Switching from Superwhisper

  1. Download Tidytalk and follow the setup guide.
  2. Copy your custom vocabulary into Settings › Vocabulary › Common Words, and your text replacements into Replacements.
  3. Instead of modes, pick a cleanup mode (Standard, Chat, Email, or Code) in Settings › Set Up AI › Tidying, and add your own instructions as Custom Rules.
  4. To keep everything local, choose Ollama, LM Studio, or Apple Intelligence as the AI service. See private and offline dictation.
