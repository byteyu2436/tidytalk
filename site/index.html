Voice input for Mac · Free and open source

Hold to talk. Let go, and you get clean text.

Tidytalk recognizes your speech on your Mac, uses AI to remove fillers like "um" and "you know" and fix slips and punctuation, then pastes the text at your cursor. It works anywhere you can type: chat apps, email, notes, and code editors.

Tidy

Three steps, no typing

No switching input methods, no fixing words one at a time.

  1. Hold Option + Command

    With your cursor in any text field, hold the shortcut. A recording indicator appears at the bottom of the screen.

  2. Say a paragraph

    Talk naturally and correct yourself as you go. Live text appears under the indicator.

  3. Let go

    The tidied text appears at your cursor, and whatever was on your clipboard is put back.

Made for people who think before they speak

It removes fillers and slips. It never summarizes or adds content, so the words stay yours.

Your voice stays on your Mac

SenseVoice recognizes speech on device and detects Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and Cantonese automatically, including English mixed into Chinese.

Tidies, doesn't rewrite

Fillers and repeats go, a corrected phrase keeps only the final version, and punctuation is added. Text that sounds like a command is tidied, not followed.

Bring your own AI

Use DeepSeek, OpenAI, or any compatible service, or a local model in Ollama or LM Studio to stay fully offline. You can also turn tidying off.

Speak one language, write another

Pick an output language and the text is translated as it's tidied: English, Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, or Spanish.

Rewrite with your voice

Select some text, hold Right Option, and say "make it more formal" or "shorten to one sentence". The selection is replaced with the rewrite.

Vocabulary and snippets

Teach it the names and terms you use, fix words it often mishears, and say "my email" to insert your full address.

Built for the way you work

Dictate prompts, emails, and notes, in the language you think in.

Set up in a minute

A short guide walks you through microphone and accessibility access, an AI service (optional), and the speech model download.

Privacy

  • Recording and speech recognition happen on your Mac. Your voice is never uploaded.
  • With Tidy up text on, the recognized text is sent to the AI service you set up. With a local model, or with tidying off, text never leaves your Mac either.
  • Settings, including your API key, are stored in ~/.tidytalk.json on your Mac, readable only by you.
  • Recent results are kept in memory only and cleared when you quit.
  • The code is fully open source, so you can read every line on GitHub.

Install

  1. Download the latest Tidytalk-x.y.z.dmg from GitHub Releases.
  2. Open it and drag Tidytalk into the Applications folder.
  3. Follow the setup guide. The speech model (about 240 MB) is downloaded the first time.

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" at the bottom, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tidytalk.app

Questions

Is Tidytalk free?

Yes. Tidytalk is free and open source under the MIT License. If you tidy text with a cloud AI service such as DeepSeek or OpenAI, you use your own API key and the provider bills you. With a local model in Ollama or LM Studio, or with tidying off, it costs nothing.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to download the speech model (about 240 MB) on first launch. After that, speech recognition works offline. Tidying with a cloud AI service needs a connection; with a local model or with tidying off, the whole app works offline.

Which languages does it support?

It detects the spoken language automatically and supports Mandarin Chinese (including mixed-in English), English, Japanese, Korean, and Cantonese. Text can also be translated into English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, or Spanish. The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

Is my voice or text uploaded?

Your voice never is. Recording and speech recognition happen on your Mac. With Tidy up text on, the recognized text is sent to the AI service you set up; with a local model or with tidying off, text never leaves your Mac either.

How is it different from macOS Dictation?

Dictation types your words as you speak. Tidytalk waits until you finish a whole paragraph, removes fillers and slips, adds punctuation, and then inserts the result in one go, which suits messages, email, and documents.

Does it run on Windows or Intel Macs?

Not yet. Tidytalk requires macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

macOS says it can't verify the developer. What should I do?

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" at the bottom. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tidytalk.app in Terminal, and it will open normally from then on.
