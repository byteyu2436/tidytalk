How it works

Three steps, in any app.

  • Hold the shortcut

    Option + Command, in any text field.

  • Speak naturally

    Pause, correct yourself, think out loud.

  • Let go

    Clean text appears right at your cursor.

What you say stays on your Mac

Your voice is always recognized on your Mac. When you want AI tidying, pick a local model and nothing goes online.

  • Speech recognition

    Always on your Mac

    The SenseVoice model runs on your Mac. Recordings are never uploaded.

  • AI tidying

    Your choice

    With Ollama, LM Studio, or Apple Intelligence, your text stays on your Mac too. With a cloud service, only the recognized text is sent.

  • Everything else

    Nothing collected

    No account and no tracking in the app. Recent results live in memory and are cleared when you quit.

Keep AI tidying on your Mac too

Run a local model with Ollama or LM Studio and Tidytalk never needs the internet to clean up your words.

  • Choose Ollama, LM Studio, Cloud, or Apple Intelligence in one click
  • No API key, and nothing sent over the internet
  • Prefer the cloud? DeepSeek, OpenAI, or any compatible API works too

It learns the words you use

Names, products, and jargon are what speech recognition gets wrong. Teach Tidytalk once.

  • Common words fix similar-sounding mistakes
  • Replacements always correct the same slip
  • Snippets turn a short phrase into your email or address

Live captions for what your Mac plays

Press ⌃⌥C to caption a video, a lecture, or a meeting. The audio is recognized on your Mac, just like your voice.

  • Listen to all audio, or just one app
  • Translate as you listen, shown under the original
  • Press ⌃⌥V to paste what was just said
  • Transcribe audio, video files, and links too

Turn recordings and videos into text

Drop in a meeting recording, a lecture, or a video, or paste a YouTube or Bilibili link. Tidytalk transcribes it on your Mac.

  • A timestamp for every sentence
  • Export as text, SRT subtitles, or Markdown
  • For links, only the audio is downloaded, then deleted

Questions

Is Tidytalk free?

Yes. Tidytalk is free and open source under the MIT License. If you tidy text with a cloud AI service such as DeepSeek or OpenAI, you use your own API key and the provider bills you. With a local model in Ollama or LM Studio, or with tidying off, it costs nothing.

Does it need an internet connection?

Speech recognition needs it only once, to download the speech model (about 240 MB) on first launch. Tidying with a cloud AI service needs a connection; with a local model or with tidying off, the whole app works offline.

Which languages does it support?

With the default SenseVoice model it detects the spoken language automatically and supports Mandarin Chinese (including mixed-in English), English, Japanese, Korean, and Cantonese. For other languages, switch to Qwen3-ASR (30 languages and 22 Chinese dialects) or Whisper Turbo (about 99) in Settings › Speech Model. Text can also be translated into English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, or Spanish. The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

Is my voice or text uploaded?

Your voice never is. Recording and speech recognition happen on your Mac. With Tidy up text on, the recognized text is sent to the AI service you set up; with a local model or with tidying off, text never leaves your Mac either.

Can it caption videos and meetings?

Yes. Press ⌃⌥C (Control + Option + C) and Tidytalk shows live captions for whatever your Mac is playing, such as a video, a lecture, or a meeting. The audio is recognized on your Mac. You can translate each sentence with your AI service, and press ⌃⌥V to tidy and paste what was just said.

Is there a free, open-source alternative to Wispr Flow for Mac?

Yes, Tidytalk. Like Wispr Flow, you hold a shortcut, speak, and get clean, punctuated text at your cursor in any app. Unlike Wispr Flow, speech is recognized on your Mac, there's no account, subscription, or weekly word limit, and the code is open source under the MIT License. See how the two compare.

How do I transcribe a meeting recording on Mac without uploading it?

Choose Transcribe File or Link from the Tidytalk menu and drop in the audio or video file, up to 3 hours long. It's recognized on your Mac and split into paragraphs with timestamps, and you can export it as TXT, SRT, or Markdown. YouTube and Bilibili links work too. Learn more about transcribing.

How is it different from macOS Dictation?

Dictation types your words as you speak. Tidytalk waits until you finish a whole paragraph, removes fillers and slips, adds punctuation, and then inserts the result in one go, which suits messages, email, and documents.

Does it run on Windows or Intel Macs?

No. Tidytalk requires macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

macOS says it can't verify the developer. What should I do?

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" at the bottom. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tidytalk.app in Terminal, and it will open normally from then on.
