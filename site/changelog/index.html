0.1.0

2026-10-16 · Download

The first preview release. Hold a shortcut and speak; let go and your words appear at the cursor. Speech is recognized on your Mac, and AI removes fillers and fixes slips and punctuation.

  • Requires macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
  • The app isn't notarized. The first time you open it, click "Open Anyway" in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tidytalk.app.
  • The speech model (about 230 MB) is downloaded on first launch.
