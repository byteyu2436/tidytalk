Dictation that never leaves your Mac

Tidytalk always recognizes your voice on your Mac. Pair it with a local model and your text stays there too.

Updated 2026-10-19

What leaves your Mac

Cloud AI service (for example DeepSeek or OpenAI)Stays on your MacSent to the AI service you chose
Local model in Ollama or LM StudioStays on your MacStays on your Mac
"Tidy up text" turned offStays on your MacStays on your Mac (raw transcript)

Speech recognition uses the SenseVoice model, which runs on your Mac after a one-time download of about 240 MB. Recent results are kept in memory only and cleared when you quit. Settings, including any API key, are stored in ~/.tidytalk.json, readable only by you.

The only other network request is the update check: at launch, at most once a day, Tidytalk reads the latest version number from GitHub. You can turn it off in Settings › General.

Set up Ollama

  1. Install Ollama.
  2. Download a model in Terminal, for example: 
    ollama pull qwen2.5:7b
  3. In Tidytalk, open Settings › Set Up AI, and choose Ollama at the top. Check that the model name matches the one you pulled, then click "Save".
  4. Click "Test Connection". When it says "Connected", you're done.

Set up LM Studio

  1. Install LM Studio, download a model, and load it.
  2. Start LM Studio's local server.
  3. In Tidytalk, choose LM Studio at the top of Settings › Set Up AI, enter the name of the loaded model, save, and click "Test Connection".

Local models don't need an API key, and Tidytalk gives them a 60-second timeout instead of 12 seconds.

Choosing a model

  • Tidying is a light task, but the model has to follow instructions: tidy what you said instead of answering it. Small models are less reliable at this and at following custom rules.
  • A model around 7B parameters or larger is a good starting point. qwen2.5:7b is about a 5 GB download and handles both Chinese and English well.
  • If the local model is slow or returns something unexpected, Tidytalk pastes the raw transcript, so you never lose what you said.

No AI at all

Turn off "Tidy up text" in Settings › Set Up AI and Tidytalk becomes a fast, fully offline speech-to-text tool. Replacements, snippets, and Chinese–English spacing still work, because they run on your Mac without AI.

Verify it yourself

Tidytalk is open source under the MIT License. You can read exactly what it sends and when on GitHub, or build it from source.
