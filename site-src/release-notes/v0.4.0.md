- A redesigned interface: the main window has four pages on the left, Home, Transcribe, Library, and Vocabulary (⌘1–⌘4), with Settings in the bottom-left corner. Every option lives in exactly one place, and empty pages tell you how to get started.
- Home shows how much you dictated today and roughly how many minutes of typing that saved, cards for Dictation, Live Captions, and Meetings, and your recent results. If a permission or the speech model is missing, a banner at the top says what's left to do.
- A new Transcribe page: drop in an audio or video file, or paste a video link, and your transcriptions are listed below. The title and cover show up as soon as Tidytalk reads them, before the audio downloads. A failed transcription can be retried in one click.
- The Library puts dictation, meeting notes, and transcriptions in one list, grouped by day, with filters, search (⌘F), and sorting. Hover over an item to copy it, tidy it again, or delete it.
- Dictation history is now saved on your Mac for 30 days by default, so you can search it in the Library. It's never uploaded, and Tidytalk doesn't record which app you pasted into. In Settings › Privacy & Data you can change how long it's kept, clear it, or turn saving off so it's cleared when you quit. If you're upgrading, the Library tells you about this once.
- Vocabulary has four tabs, Terms, Replacements, Snippets, and Custom Rules, and changes save right away. Words you correct after pasting are suggested for your vocabulary.
- Settings are grouped into Features, Shared, and System. Settings › General adds Appearance (System, Light, Dark) and Language (System, 简体中文, English).
- Meeting details can play back the whole meeting, with your side and the other side together, and you can scrub and change the speed. Settings › Meetings adds a switch for telling speakers apart. During a meeting, both audio sources show a level meter, and Tidytalk tells you if it keeps hearing nothing from your Mac.
- Detail pages have a back button in the top-left corner (⌘[), and long titles wrap. Meetings and transcriptions can be exported with File › Export… (⇧⌘E).
- Property labels on meetings and transcriptions are no longer cut off in English.
