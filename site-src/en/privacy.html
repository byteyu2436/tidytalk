Privacy

Tidytalk needs no account and has no analytics, tracking, ads, or crash reporting. Your voice is always recognized on your Mac. This page explains what Tidytalk stores on your Mac, when it goes online, and which text is sent where.

{{updated}}

In short

  • Your voice is never uploaded. Dictation, live captions, meeting notes, and file transcription are all recognized on this Mac.
  • Text only goes to the AI service you choose. With a local model (Ollama, LM Studio) or Apple Intelligence, text stays on this Mac too. With "Tidy up text" turned off, you get the raw transcript.
  • The developer never sees your audio, text, or usage. Tidytalk has no servers of its own.

Audio

  • Dictation: Tidytalk records only while you hold the shortcut (or after you double-tap it for hands-free recording). The recording stays in memory, is discarded after recognition, and is never written to disk.
  • Live captions: Tidytalk listens to your Mac's audio. The audio and caption text stay in memory and are cleared when you turn captions off or quit.
  • Meeting notes: your microphone and your Mac's audio are recorded, and the recordings and transcripts are saved on this Mac (see below). You can delete just the recording and keep the text, or delete the whole meeting, and you can turn off "Keep recordings after the meeting" in Settings.
  • File and link transcription: recognized on this Mac.

Text sent to an AI service

Text is sent only when you turn on the feature and choose a service in Settings › Set Up AI:

  • Tidy up text: the recognized text, plus the terms in your vocabulary and your custom rules, so the AI follows them.
  • Voice rewrite: the text you selected and the change you asked for.
  • Caption translation: each caption, with a few earlier ones for context.
  • Meeting summary: the meeting transcript and the speakers' names. Summaries aren't automatic by default: text is sent only when you click the purple Summarize button, and Tidytalk shows which service will receive it.

Text sent to a cloud service is handled under that service's own privacy policy. Your API key is kept in the macOS Keychain and is sent only to the endpoint you entered.

When downloading files or checking for updates, the other side can see your IP address, as with any network request. Tidytalk doesn't go online in any other case.

~/Library/Application Support/Tidytalk/Speech models, the speaker model, and yt-dlp; meeting notes (Meetings/, transcripts and recordings); transcriptions (Transcriptions/, text plus the audio and cover images downloaded from links). For a file on your Mac, only its location is saved, not a copy
app.tidytalk.Tidytalk in system preferencesWindow positions, whether you've seen the setup guide, and when updates were last checked

Meeting and transcription files are readable only by your user account. Your last 20 dictation results and live captions are kept in memory only and cleared when you quit Tidytalk.

You can delete meetings and transcriptions one by one in the main window. To remove everything, follow Uninstall in the user guide.

Pasting briefly uses the clipboard. As soon as the target app has read it, your previous clipboard contents (text, images, or files) are put back.

This website

tidytalk.zayen.dev is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individuals. It records only totals, such as which pages were viewed, which site sent the visit, the browser, and the country or region. GitHub and Cloudflare handle their access logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies (GitHub, Cloudflare). Downloads and update checks also go through GitHub.
